Sêva Sor – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

App: Sêva Sor · Developer: Nizar Sadiq (N|SADIQ)

English

We collect almost nothing. We never ask for your name, email, phone number, location, contacts, photos or any other personal information.

  • No account. You do not register or log in. The game signs you in anonymously: Google Firebase creates a random ID that has no link to your name or your Google account.
  • What is stored on our server (Google Firebase, Realtime Database, region Belgium/EU): this random ID, the village you chose in the game and the time you chose it. It is used only to count how many players each village has, so that more trucks from bigger villages arrive in the game. A village cannot be changed later.
  • Your progress (coins, buildings, settings, language) is saved only on your device. It is deleted when you uninstall the app or reset the game. A backup file is created only if you press the backup button, and it stays on your phone unless you choose to share it.
  • Notifications are created on your phone (local). Nothing is sent to a server for them. You can switch them off in the phone settings.
  • The game has no ad network, no analytics, no in-app purchases and no third-party tracking. It may show sponsor billboards (marked “Ad”); a link opens in your browser only when you tap it.
  • When online, the game contacts Google Firebase (sign-in, village counts, announcements, sponsor texts) and image servers. Like every web request, these servers can see your IP address. We do not use it and do not store it.
  • Sharing: we do not sell or share data. Google Firebase processes the data for us as our service provider.
  • Deletion: write to the developer, Nizar Sadiq, at the address below and we delete your anonymous player entry and take you out of the village count. Please send the player ID shown in the game Settings, or tell us your village and the approximate date you chose it. Uninstalling the app removes all data on your phone.
  • The game is intended for users aged 13 and over.
